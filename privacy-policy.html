Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ResumeBuild ("we", "our" or "the website") handles information when you visit or use our resume builder and review tools.

1. Information You Provide

  • Account information such as your email address and display name when you register or sign in.
  • Resume details you enter in the resume builder, such as contact details, work experience, education and skills.
  • Resume content and an optional job description if you choose the AI rewrite feature.
  • Messages and information you send when contacting support.

2. How Resume Files Are Processed

For the basic resume review, supported PDF, DOCX and TXT files are read and analysed in your browser. This basic review feature does not upload the selected file or its extracted text to our server.

If you choose to save a resume while signed in, the resume details you entered are stored in our Firebase Firestore database under your account so that the save feature can work. Do not save information you do not want associated with your account.

3. Optional AI Resume Rewrite

The AI rewrite feature is optional and requires you to sign in and actively tick a consent checkbox before submitting. When you use it, the extracted resume text and any job description you provide are sent to our Cloudflare-hosted backend and then to OpenAI to generate suggested wording. The backend verifies your Firebase sign-in token before processing the request.

The backend sends the AI request with storage disabled (store: false) and is designed not to save or log the submitted resume text or AI response. OpenAI processes the submitted content under its applicable terms and privacy policies. Please review those policies before using this optional feature. Always check generated suggestions for accuracy.

4. Authentication and Service Providers

We use Firebase Authentication to support email/password and Google sign-in. We use Firebase Firestore for resume details that a signed-in user chooses to save. We use Cloudflare to host the website and process the optional AI rewrite request. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own applicable terms and policies.

5. Analytics and Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve the service. Analytics technologies may collect information such as pages visited, browser type, device information and approximate usage details. Google may process this information under its own privacy terms. Your browser settings may allow you to control cookies.

6. Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies. You can manage ad personalisation through Google's ad settings.

7. How We Use Information

  • To provide account sign-in and resume-building features.
  • To store resumes that you choose to save.
  • To provide the optional AI rewrite after your consent.
  • To respond to support requests and maintain website security.
  • To understand site usage and improve functionality.

8. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Saved resume information may remain in your account until it is deleted through an available feature or you contact us to request assistance. Some service providers may retain limited technical or account records under their own policies.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You can choose not to use the optional AI rewrite feature. You can also choose not to save resume information to an account. For privacy questions or requests relating to information associated with your use of the website, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our features or practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact Us
