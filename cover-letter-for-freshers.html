Cover Letter Guide for Freshers in India

A cover letter is a short message that explains why you are applying and what relevant evidence you can bring. It should add context to your resume, not repeat every line. Some employers do not request one; follow the vacancy instructions and do not send unnecessary attachments.

A simple four-part structure

  1. Opening: Name the role and where you found it.
  2. Relevant background: Mention your degree, training or experience that matches the role.
  3. Evidence: Give one specific example from a project, internship or activity.
  4. Closing: Refer to the attached resume and thank the reader.

Example for a fresher

Subject: Application for [Job Title] – [Your Name]

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the [Job Title] position at [Company], as advertised on [source]. I recently completed [degree] at [college/university].

During [project, internship or coursework], I worked on [specific task] using [relevant tools or skills]. This helped me develop practical experience in [relevant area], which I would like to build on in this role.

I have attached my resume for your consideration. Thank you for reviewing my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my suitability.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email]

Make it specific

Before sending, read the job description and identify one or two requirements that match your real background. Mention the company or role accurately. Avoid generic statements such as “your esteemed organisation” without explaining why the role interests you.

Common mistakes

  • Sending the same letter to every employer without checking the role.
  • Repeating the resume word for word.
  • Making unsupported claims about expertise or achievements.
  • Writing several pages when a short letter is enough.
  • Leaving placeholders or the wrong company name in the final copy.

When a cover letter is not requested

If the application portal does not ask for a cover letter, do not assume one is required. If there is an optional message field, a concise note may be suitable. Always respect the employer's stated process.
