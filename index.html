HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ResumeBuild.

What is ResumeBuild?

An online tool to review an existing resume, compare it with a job description, and create a professional resume.

Which file formats can I upload?

The resume review supports PDF, DOCX and TXT files up to 8 MB.

Can I create a resume from scratch?

Yes. Open Resume Builder and enter your personal details, experience, education and skills.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Select Continue with Google in the Login window, or use email and password.

Is my uploaded resume sent to your server?

The basic resume review reads the selected file in your browser and does not upload it to our server for that review. See our Privacy Policy for other features.

Does the review guarantee a job interview?

No. It provides general suggestions and cannot guarantee an interview or job offer.
