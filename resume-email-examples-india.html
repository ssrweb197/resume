How to Email Your Resume for a Job Application

A job application email should make it easy for a recruiter to understand which role you are applying for and find your attached resume. Keep it brief, polite and specific. Follow the employer's instructions if they request a particular subject line or application portal.

Subject line examples

  • Application for Graduate Engineer Trainee – Your Name
  • Application for Junior Software Developer – Your Name
  • Resume Submission – Marketing Associate – Your Name
  • Application for [Exact Job Title] – [Your Name] – [Job ID, if provided]

Email example for a fresher

Subject: Application for [Job Title] – [Your Name]

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the [Job Title] position advertised on [source]. I recently completed [degree] at [college/university] and have worked on academic projects involving [relevant skills or tools].

I have attached my resume for your consideration. I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how my background aligns with the role.

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email Address]

Email example for an experienced applicant

Subject: Application for [Job Title] – [Your Name]

Dear [Recruiter's Name / Hiring Team],

I would like to apply for the [Job Title] role at [Company]. My experience in [relevant area] includes [one accurate, role-related achievement or responsibility].

Please find my resume attached for your review. I would be glad to provide any additional information required.

Thank you for your consideration.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email Address]

Before sending

  1. Check the recipient address and exact job title.
  2. Use a professional email address you check regularly.
  3. Attach the correct final resume as PDF unless another format is requested.
  4. Name the file clearly, for example YourName_Resume.pdf.
  5. Open the attachment once to make sure it is readable and complete.
  6. Remove all placeholders before sending.
  7. Do not attach identity documents, bank details or unrelated personal files unless a legitimate process specifically requires them.

Following up

If the employer has not given a response timeline, a short, polite follow-up after a reasonable interval may be appropriate. Avoid repeated messages. If the vacancy notice says not to follow up, respect that instruction.

Prepare your resume

Use the ResumeGuide builder and review tool to prepare your resume. Check all dates, contact details and claims yourself. These examples are general writing guidance and do not guarantee a response or interview.
