About ResumeGuide

ResumeGuide is an independent online project that provides practical tools and educational guidance for people preparing job applications. It is designed for students, fresh graduates and working professionals who want to organise their resume information and review it before applying.

What the website currently provides

  • Resume builder: Enter contact details, a professional summary, work experience, education and skills, then view a formatted resume and use the browser print dialog to save it as a PDF.
  • Basic resume review: Upload a supported PDF, DOCX or TXT file (up to 8 MB) for an automated checklist. Basic file extraction and review run in the browser.
  • Job-description keyword comparison: Compare words in a resume with a job description. This is a simple text comparison, not an employer's applicant-tracking system or a certified score.
  • Optional AI rewrite: Where configured and available, a signed-in user can explicitly consent to send resume text and an optional job description for AI-generated wording suggestions.
  • Career guides: Read practical articles about preparing a fresher resume, describing projects and sending job applications.

Our approach

We aim to make the resume preparation process easier to understand. We encourage users to provide accurate information, describe only skills and achievements they can support, and check every suggested change before sharing a resume.

Important limitations

Our tools provide general assistance. They do not represent an employer, recruiter or official ATS provider, and they cannot guarantee an interview, job offer or hiring outcome. Some features may change or be temporarily unavailable.
